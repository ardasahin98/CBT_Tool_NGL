Analyst models

CBT is not measured; it is a judgment. The tool reproduces the judgments of ten analysts with one model each and combines them, so the result carries both each analyst's uncertainty and their disagreement.

The analysts

Ten experienced analysts independently assigned a CBT between 0 (clay-like) and 1 (sand-like) to each of 195 CDSS tests from the NGL database. They are identified by their initials: AJ, AS, AWS, JPS, KJU, KOC, SJB, SLK, TME and VR. Every model uses Ratiohull and ru,max; some analysts' judgments are also explained by the strain accumulation curvature κγ or by RatioΔγ.

Selecting analysts

The Analyst models box lists the ten models, each with its colour in the plots. All are ticked by default. Untick an analyst to leave their model out of the combined CBT; All and None tick or untick every model. The results, plots and downloads update immediately, and the downloads record which analysts were used.

The selected analysts are combined with equal weights (1 / number selected). With one analyst selected, the result is that analyst's model alone and has no between-analyst uncertainty.

Use all ten unless you have a reason not to

The spread between analysts is a real part of the uncertainty in CBT. Leaving analysts out narrows the reported range without making the estimate more accurate.
