Reading the results

What each box and plot shows, which cycle to read CBT at, and what the yellow caution means.

The file list

Every loaded test is listed in box 2 with a chip showing its state: queued, computing…, error (hover for the message), or its combined CBT at the last cycle. A yellow chip means the test did not reach γDA = 9%. Click a name to show that test; × removes it.

Test summary and cycle

The box at the top left gives the file name (and the NGL identifiers for NGL tests) and a summary of the whole record:

ItemMeaning
CyclesNumber of loading cycles detected. A cycle starts where the shear stress crosses zero from negative to positive.
σ′v0Vertical effective stress at the start of cyclic loading (first row of the file).
CSRLargest cyclic stress ratio, τ/σ′v0, over the test.
max γDALargest double-amplitude shear strain.
γDA=9% cycleFirst cycle with γDA ≥ 9%, or not reached.
Ns (γSA=3%)Number of cycles to a single-amplitude strain of 3%, interpolated within the cycle; the last cycle if 3% is not reached.

Below it, choose the cycle at which CBT and the metrics are shown: the slider, ◀ ▶, a cycle number, Last cycle, or First γDA ≥ 9%. The line underneath gives γDA, γSA and CSR of the selected cycle.

Which cycle to use

By default the tool shows the last cycle. Ultimate hysteretic behavior tends to have developed by the cycle in which γDA reaches 9%, so that cycle is marked in every plot and can be selected with one click. For a test carried well past 9%, comparing the two shows whether CBT is still changing.

The γDA = 9% caution

If a test stops before γDA = 9%, CBT is still reported, but a yellow caution is shown on every tab, the chip in the file list turns yellow, and the downloads carry the same note. Such a test may have stopped before its ultimate hysteretic behavior and susceptibility developed, so be careful when inferring them from it.

Combined CBT

The large number is the median of the combined CBT distribution at the selected cycle. The gauge runs from 0 (clay-like) to 1 (sand-like); the dark line is the median and the grey band the 16th–84th percentile range.

The table under the gauge:

  • Median, Mean: the median is expit(μT); the mean differs because the logit transformation is not linear.
  • 16–84%, 5–95%: percentile ranges, expit(μT ± σT) and expit(μT ± 1.645σT).
  • μT, σT: mean and standard deviation of logit(CBT), the space in which the models work.
  • σ within: how uncertain each analyst's own estimate is. σ between: how much the analysts disagree. σT² is the sum of their squares.
  • Analysts: how many of the ten analyst models are combined (see Analyst models).

The uncertainty is wide by design: it carries both the scatter of each analyst's judgments and the disagreement between analysts. Read CBT with its range, not only the median.

Metrics

The four predictors at the selected cycle: ru,max, Ratiohull, RatioΔγ, and κγ (one value for the whole record). They are defined in Metrics and CBT models. Hover over a value for a short description.

Test record tab

  • Stress–strain loops (top left): all cycles in grey, the selected cycle in black, and its convex hull in orange. Ratiohull is the loop area divided by the hull area: a pinched, banana-shaped loop fills less of its hull.
  • Stress path (top middle): τ/σ′v0 against σ′v/σ′v0.
  • Strain history (bottom left) and vertical effective stress history, σ′v/σ′v0 = 1 − ru (bottom middle), with time on the vertical axis and reference lines at ru = 0.90, 0.95 and 1.0.
  • Strain accumulation (right): γSA/γs against N/Ns. The red points at 10%, 50% and 90% of the record give κγ; the blue diamond is the selected cycle.

Metrics vs. cycle tab

How ru,max, Ratiohull, RatioΔγ and γDA develop over the test. The solid black line is the selected cycle, the dashed green line the first cycle with γDA ≥ 9%.

CBT tab

  • Left: CBT of each analyst model (small dots) and the combined median (large dots) against γDA, for every cycle. This shows how the estimate develops as the test proceeds.
  • Right: the probability density of CBT at the selected cycle, for each analyst and combined. Tick Distribution in logit space to see them as the normal distributions the models produce.
  • Show individual analysts turns the per-analyst curves on and off.

Analyst results tab

One row per analyst model: its predictors, μT and σT, the two parts of σT (σ coef., from the uncertainty of the coefficients, and σ̂ model, the model's own scatter), the median CBT, the 16–84% range and its weight. The last row is the combination. Unticked analysts are greyed out.
